# Testing & Validation

Real engineering case studies on building **confidence before production changes**. Each article follows an operational problem through risk discovery, lab design, observable test cases, failure analysis, rollback planning and an evidence-backed production-readiness decision.

Rather than publishing a checklist of commands alone, this section explains **what to test, why it matters, how to measure the result, and what evidence a customer needs**.

## The repeatable framework

**Define the change → model the failure domains → agree acceptance criteria → instrument the baseline → exercise normal and failure paths → verify recovery → report evidence and residual risks.**

Future case studies can reuse this structure for ACI upgrades, Nexus maintenance, CIMC/UCS upgrades, network automation and agentic validation.
